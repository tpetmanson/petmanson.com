The autumn leaves are falling again, marking the end of yet another year. The world is painted in fabulous yellow, orange and red tones and cool wind gently breezes and roams around the city.

A perfect time to make another song, this time with Benjamin Börchinger. We made an ultra-groovy and ultra smooth lo-fi jazz song called "Trumimasen", which is a mix between Estonian "trummimasin" and Japanese "sumimasen".

Hope you like the smooth vibes :) Enjoy!
