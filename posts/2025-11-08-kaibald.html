Kaibaldi liivik on kõige suurem liivik Eestis. See asub Kaibaldi nõmmel Hiiumaal, Kanapeeksi küla lähedal. Liivik on Pihla-Kaibaldi looduskaitseala Kaibaldi sihtkaitsevööndi keskosaks. Liivik on inimtekkeline: II maailmasõja ajal põlenud metsaala kasutati hiljem tankide õppeväljakuna.

Appearantly Estonia does have one sandy desert, who would have guessed that? Interestingly enough it is located in Hiiumaa, right where two founding members Don and Liisi from Kaibald band are also from.

Photos by Hando Luukas

Since the beginning of this year, I've been hanging out with these really hard-working and talented musicians on producing together a awesome alt.western/blues/rock album and we are going to record it next February right here in Tallinn. This stuff is going to blow you away for sure!

We have fourteen new songs and are currently putting together a line-up of musicians to record the album. This is going to be a really lovely vibing experience for the listener, we got some blues, we got rock, we got western sounding epic themes, we got the trumpet, we got the keys, we got it all!
