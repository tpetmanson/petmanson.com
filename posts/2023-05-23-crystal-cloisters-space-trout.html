When I was still in Switzerland, I recorded some keyboard parts for Crystal Cloisters new album "Space trout", which they've now released in 2023

It is funny that I have not even once played with them together, not even in a jam, yet I've participated on two of their albums.

Funny story: before I met one of the guitarists Karlis, I actually had gone to his place to fix his computer that his friend Punkar "Porno" had crashed. The computer got fixed and Porno was visibly relieved. Obviously I noticed bunch of guitars and music equipment lying around, even an electronic drumset.

My neighbour Marko later introduced us and I've seen Karlis around in underground rehearsal places in Tartu since.

Anyways, get the album from bandcamp while its hot.
