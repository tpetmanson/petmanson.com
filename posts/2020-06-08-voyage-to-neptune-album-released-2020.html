2020-06-08
« PreviousMain pageNext »

Hallo Leute! Tere maakera! I am thrilled to present to you "Voyage to Neptune", a collection of songs recorded in 2019 using live-looping technique with a keyboard and a drum machine, each song recorded in a single take.

You will taste mostly jazzy, funky electronic music with occasional reggae and a tons of edgy keyboard solos.
